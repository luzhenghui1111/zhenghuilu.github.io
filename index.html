About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of British Columbia. My research focuses on human biomechanics, musculoskeletal modeling, finite element analysis, artificial intelligence, and additive manufacturing.

My long-term research goal is to develop individualized digital biomechanics and precision musculoskeletal rehabilitation approaches for predicting patient-specific biomechanical responses and supporting personalized intervention design.

Google Scholar ORCID GitHub Email

Research Interests

Human Biomechanics

Gait analysis, joint loading, movement assessment, muscle activation, and lower-limb biomechanics.

Musculoskeletal Modeling

OpenSim-based musculoskeletal simulation and subject-specific biomechanical modeling.

Finite Element Modeling

Patient-specific finite element analysis of musculoskeletal structures and biomechanical loading.

AI & Personalized Rehabilitation

Machine learning, surrogate modeling, digital biomechanics, and personalized intervention optimization.
