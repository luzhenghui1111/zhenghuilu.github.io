Personalized digital biomechanics for musculoskeletal health面向肌肉骨骼健康的个体化数字生物力学

I integrate experimental biomechanics, musculoskeletal modeling, finite element analysis, machine learning, and additive manufacturing to understand internal musculoskeletal loading and develop individualized interventions.

我将实验生物力学、肌肉骨骼建模、有限元分析、机器学习与增材制造相结合，用于理解肌肉骨骼内部负荷并发展个体化干预方法。

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Physical Therapy, Faculty of Medicine, at the University of British Columbia. My postdoctoral work focuses on knee osteoarthritis, patient-specific joint loading, and precision non-surgical rehabilitation. My doctoral research developed data-driven methods for personalized lattice footwear and biomechanical optimization.

我目前是加拿大英属哥伦比亚大学医学部物理治疗系博士后研究员。博士后阶段主要聚焦膝骨关节炎、患者特异性关节内部负荷与精准非手术康复；博士阶段则围绕数据驱动的个体化晶格鞋具设计与生物力学优化开展研究。

Research Focus研究主线From measurement to intervention从实验测量到个体化干预

01

Personalized Musculoskeletal Biomechanics个体化肌肉骨骼生物力学

Subject-specific gait and movement analysis, OpenSim musculoskeletal modeling, joint-contact-force estimation, and finite element analysis of internal tissue mechanics.

结合个体化步态与动作分析、OpenSim 肌肉骨骼建模、关节接触力估计和有限元分析研究内部组织力学。

02

AI-Driven Footwear & Additive Manufacturing人工智能驱动的鞋具与增材制造

Data-driven optimization of footwear and lattice structures using plantar-pressure data, finite element simulation, machine learning, and 3D printing.

基于足底压力、有限元仿真、机器学习和 3D 打印开展鞋具及晶格结构的数据驱动优化。

03

Precision Rehabilitation & Knee Osteoarthritis精准康复与膝骨关节炎

Current postdoctoral work investigates individualized loading patterns and heterogeneous responses to gait and footwear interventions in knee osteoarthritis.

当前博士后研究聚焦膝骨关节炎患者的个体化负荷模式，以及对步态和鞋具干预的差异化响应。

Featured Research重点研究

Dual-layer lattice

Computationally tuned dual-layer lattice pads adapted to gait-induced pressure distribution

npj Advanced Manufacturing · 2025 · First author

Integrates finite element analysis, Gaussian-process surrogate modeling, and Bayesian optimization to tailor dual-layer lattice pads to gait-induced plantar-pressure patterns.

融合有限元、高斯过程代理模型和贝叶斯优化，根据步态足底压力分布定制双层晶格垫。

FEA × ML insole

Parametric cushioning lattice insole based on finite element method and machine learning

Journal of Biomechanics · 2025 · First author

Develops a controllable parametric lattice insole and couples finite element simulation with machine learning to optimize cushioning and plantar-pressure reduction.

构建可控参数化晶格鞋垫，并结合有限元和机器学习优化缓冲性能与足底压力降低。

3D printing review

Will this be the next step? A systematic review of 3D printing in footwear biomechanics

Footwear Science · 2025 · First author

Synthesizes evidence on 3D-printed footwear for injury prevention, comfort, and sports performance, and identifies directions for personalized design.

系统总结 3D 打印鞋具在损伤预防、舒适性和运动表现方面的证据，并提出个体化设计的发展方向。
