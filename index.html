I connect experimental biomechanics, musculoskeletal modeling, finite element analysis, machine learning, and additive manufacturing to understand internal loading and develop individualized interventions.

我将实验生物力学、肌肉骨骼建模、有限元分析、机器学习与增材制造连接起来，用于理解内部负荷并发展个体化干预方法。

My current postdoctoral work at UBC focuses on knee osteoarthritis, patient-specific joint loading, and precision non-surgical rehabilitation. My doctoral research developed data-driven methods for personalized lattice footwear and biomechanical optimization.

目前在 UBC 的博士后研究聚焦膝骨关节炎、患者特异性关节负荷和精准非手术康复；博士阶段则围绕数据驱动的个体化晶格鞋具设计与生物力学优化展开。

CURRENT AT UBC目前在 UBC

From personalized models to precision rehabilitation.从个体化模型走向精准康复。

My postdoctoral work extends the personalized digital-biomechanics framework to knee osteoarthritis. I am interested in estimating internal knee loading, building patient-specific musculoskeletal and finite element models, and predicting why individuals respond differently to gait and footwear interventions.

我的博士后研究将个体化数字生物力学框架拓展到膝骨关节炎，重点关注膝关节内部负荷估计、患者特异性肌肉骨骼与有限元建模，以及不同个体对步态和鞋具干预产生不同响应的原因。
