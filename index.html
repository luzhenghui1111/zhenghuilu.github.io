About Me关于我

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Physical Therapy, Faculty of Medicine, at the University of British Columbia. My work combines experimental biomechanics, musculoskeletal modeling, finite element analysis, artificial intelligence, and additive manufacturing.

My current research focuses on knee osteoarthritis, patient-specific internal joint loading, and precision rehabilitation. More broadly, I am interested in using digital biomechanics to understand how people move, why individuals respond differently to intervention, and how computational tools can support personalized design and clinical decision-making.

我目前是加拿大英属哥伦比亚大学医学部物理治疗系博士后研究员。我的研究融合实验生物力学、肌肉骨骼建模、有限元分析、人工智能与增材制造。

目前主要聚焦膝骨关节炎、患者特异性关节内部负荷与精准康复。更广泛地说，我希望利用数字生物力学理解人体运动、解释不同个体对干预产生不同响应的原因，并发展支持个体化设计和临床决策的计算工具。

Computationally tuned dual-layer lattice pads adapted to gait-induced pressure distribution

npj Advanced Manufacturing · 2025

This study combines finite element simulation, Gaussian-process surrogate modeling and Bayesian optimization to tailor a dual-layer lattice pad to individual gait-induced plantar-pressure patterns, providing a practical route from pressure data to personalized manufacturable cushioning.

本研究将有限元仿真、高斯过程代理模型和贝叶斯优化结合，根据个体步态足底压力分布优化双层晶格垫，展示了从压力数据到可制造个体化缓冲结构的设计流程。

Parametric cushioning lattice insole based on finite element method and machine learning

Journal of Biomechanics · 2025

A parameterized lattice insole was evaluated through finite element simulations and machine-learning prediction, linking structural parameters with cushioning behavior and enabling rapid screening of promising designs for plantar-pressure reduction.

本研究构建参数化晶格鞋垫，通过有限元仿真与机器学习建立结构参数和缓冲性能之间的关系，从而能够快速筛选更有利于降低足底压力的设计方案。

Will this be the next step? A systematic review of 3D printing in footwear biomechanics

Footwear Science · 2025

This systematic review synthesizes current evidence on 3D-printed footwear for injury prevention, comfort and athletic performance, highlighting the promise of personalized design while identifying major gaps in validation and methodological consistency.

本系统综述整合 3D 打印鞋具在损伤预防、舒适性和运动表现方面的研究证据，指出个体化设计具有较大潜力，同时现有研究在验证和方法一致性方面仍存在不足。
