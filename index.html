About Me 关于我

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Physical Therapy, Faculty of Medicine, at the University of British Columbia.

My research focuses on human biomechanics, musculoskeletal modeling, finite element analysis, artificial intelligence, and additive manufacturing. I am particularly interested in integrating experimental biomechanics, computational modeling, and data-driven approaches to better understand musculoskeletal loading and develop personalized interventions.

My long-term research goal is to develop individualized digital biomechanics and precision musculoskeletal rehabilitation approaches that can support patient-specific prediction, intervention design, and clinical decision-making.

我目前是加拿大英属哥伦比亚大学医学部物理治疗系的博士后研究员。

我的研究主要聚焦于人体生物力学、肌肉骨骼建模、有限元分析、 人工智能以及增材制造。我尤其关注如何将实验生物力学、 计算建模与数据驱动方法相结合，以更好地理解肌肉骨骼系统负荷， 并开发个体化干预方案。

我的长期研究目标是发展个体化数字生物力学与精准肌肉骨骼康复方法， 用于支持患者特异性预测、干预设计以及临床决策。

Research Interests 研究方向

Human Biomechanics 人体生物力学

Gait analysis, joint loading, movement assessment, neuromuscular function, and lower-limb biomechanics.

步态分析、关节负荷、运动评估、神经肌肉功能以及下肢生物力学。

Musculoskeletal Modeling 肌肉骨骼建模

OpenSim-based simulation, subject-specific modeling, joint contact force estimation, and biomechanical analysis.

基于 OpenSim 的肌肉骨骼仿真、个体化建模、 关节接触力估计以及生物力学分析。

Finite Element Modeling 有限元建模

Subject-specific finite element modeling of musculoskeletal structures, tissue stress and strain analysis, and model simplification.

肌肉骨骼结构个体化有限元建模、组织应力与应变分析， 以及有限元模型简化与优化。

AI & Personalized Rehabilitation 人工智能与个体化康复

Machine learning, surrogate modeling, digital biomechanics, personalized intervention prediction, and optimization.

机器学习、代理模型、数字生物力学、 个体化干预效果预测以及优化设计。

Selected Projects 主要研究项目

Personalized Knee OA Biomechanics 膝骨关节炎个体化生物力学

Integrating gait analysis, musculoskeletal modeling, finite element simulation, and AI to investigate individualized biomechanical responses in knee osteoarthritis.

融合步态分析、肌肉骨骼建模、有限元仿真与人工智能， 研究膝骨关节炎患者的个体化生物力学响应。

Personalized 3D-Printed Footwear 个体化 3D 打印鞋具

Developing data-driven approaches for optimizing personalized lattice structures and footwear interventions using finite element modeling, machine learning, and additive manufacturing.

基于有限元建模、机器学习和增材制造， 开发数据驱动的个体化晶格结构与鞋具干预优化方法。
