Research program研究体系

Research研究方向

My work connects experimental human biomechanics with computational modeling and data-driven methods. The long-term goal is to develop patient-specific digital biomechanics tools that can support prediction, intervention design, and precision musculoskeletal rehabilitation.

我的研究将人体实验生物力学、计算建模与数据驱动方法连接起来，长期目标是发展患者特异性的数字生物力学工具，用于支持预测、干预设计和精准肌肉骨骼康复。

Research Themes核心研究主题

Personalization

Patient-Specific Musculoskeletal Biomechanics患者特异性肌肉骨骼生物力学

I use motion capture, force and pressure measurements, OpenSim-based musculoskeletal models, medical-image reconstruction, and finite element analysis to estimate internal joint and tissue mechanics that cannot be measured directly in vivo.

我结合动作捕捉、力学与足底压力测量、OpenSim 肌肉骨骼模型、医学影像重建及有限元分析，估计人体内无法直接测量的关节和组织内部力学。

AI + Modeling

Data-Driven Surrogate Modeling & Optimization数据驱动代理模型与优化

My computational work uses parameterized FE models, machine learning, surrogate models, and optimization algorithms to replace repeated high-cost simulation with rapid prediction and design exploration.

我的计算研究利用参数化有限元、机器学习、代理模型与优化算法，将重复高成本仿真转化为快速预测与设计搜索。

Translation

Footwear, Lattice Structures & Additive Manufacturing鞋具、晶格结构与增材制造

A major research line translates gait and plantar-pressure data into personalized lattice footwear using finite element modeling, intelligent optimization, and 3D printing.

该研究主线将步态与足底压力数据转化为个体化晶格鞋具，通过有限元、智能优化和 3D 打印实现计算设计到实际产品的连接。

Current Postdoctoral Direction当前博士后研究方向

Knee osteoarthritis

Individualized Internal Knee Loading个体化膝关节内部负荷

At UBC, I am extending my previous modeling framework toward patient-oriented knee osteoarthritis research. This includes musculoskeletal estimation of total and compartmental knee loading, high-fidelity finite element simulation, and rapid surrogate prediction of internal mechanical outcomes.

在 UBC，我将既有建模框架拓展到患者导向的膝骨关节炎研究，包括膝关节总负荷与内外侧分区负荷估计、高保真有限元仿真，以及内部力学结果的快速代理预测。

Precision rehabilitation

Heterogeneous Response to Non-Surgical Intervention非手术干预的个体差异响应

A second goal is to understand why patients respond differently to gait retraining, footwear, and related non-surgical interventions, and to use biomechanical and computational features to support individualized intervention selection.

另一目标是解释不同患者为什么对步态再训练、鞋具及其他非手术干预产生不同响应，并利用生物力学和计算特征支持个体化干预选择。

Research Development研究发展路径

2020–2023

Experimental + Computational Lower-Limb Biomechanics实验与计算下肢生物力学

During my master's training at Ningbo University, I combined experimental movement analysis with OpenSim and finite element modeling across gait, squatting, jumping, fatigue, footwear, and lower-limb musculoskeletal problems.

硕士阶段在宁波大学将人体实验、OpenSim 和有限元方法结合，用于步态、深蹲、跳跃、疲劳、鞋具和下肢肌肉骨骼问题。

2023–2026

Data-Driven Personalized Footwear数据驱动个体化鞋具

My PhD research established a pipeline connecting gait-induced plantar pressure, parameterized FE modeling, ML-based prediction, lattice design, and additive manufacturing. This work was developed through international and industrial collaboration.

博士研究建立了连接步态足底压力、参数化有限元、机器学习预测、晶格设计及增材制造的研发流程，并通过国际和产学合作推进。

2026–Present

Precision Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation精准肌肉骨骼康复

At UBC, the same personalized digital-biomechanics framework is being extended to knee osteoarthritis and clinically relevant prediction of patient-specific intervention response.

在 UBC，我将同一个体化数字生物力学框架进一步拓展到膝骨关节炎和具有临床意义的患者干预响应预测。
