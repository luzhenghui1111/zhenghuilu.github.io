Projects研究项目

Selected projects illustrate how my work moves from experimental measurement to computational modeling, rapid prediction, design optimization, and translation into rehabilitation or product-development settings.

以下项目展示了我的研究如何从实验测量进入计算建模、快速预测、设计优化，并进一步转化到康复或产品研发场景。

Current Research当前研究

UBC · Postdoctoral research

Personalized Knee Osteoarthritis Biomechanics膝骨关节炎个体化生物力学

Developing a patient-specific framework that links gait and clinical data with musculoskeletal and finite element models to estimate total and compartmental knee loading.

建立连接步态、临床数据、肌肉骨骼模型和有限元模型的患者特异性框架，用于估计膝关节总负荷及内外侧分区负荷。

  • OpenSim-based joint loading and muscle-force estimation基于 OpenSim 的关节负荷与肌力估计
  • Subject-specific high-fidelity knee FE modeling患者特异性高保真膝关节有限元
  • Validation using instrumented-knee and public datasets where appropriate结合 instrumented knee 等公开数据进行验证
UBC · Precision rehabilitation

Predicting Response to Gait & Footwear Interventions步态与鞋具干预响应预测

Using experimental and simulated biomechanics to identify factors associated with heterogeneous response to non-surgical knee OA interventions.

利用实验与仿真生物力学识别膝骨关节炎患者对非手术干预产生不同响应的关键因素。

  • Multimodal clinical and biomechanical features多模态临床与生物力学特征
  • FE-derived internal-mechanics outcomes有限元得到的内部力学结果
  • AI surrogate models for rapid individualized prediction用于快速个体化预测的 AI 代理模型

Doctoral Research & Industry Translation博士研究与产学转化

University of Pannonia × ANTA

Data-Driven Personalized Lattice Footwear Design数据驱动个体化晶格鞋具设计

My doctoral program developed a workflow that converts individualized gait-induced plantar pressure into parameterized lattice designs using FE simulation, machine learning, optimization, and additive manufacturing.

博士阶段建立了将个体步态足底压力转化为参数化晶格结构的研发流程，融合有限元仿真、机器学习、优化算法与增材制造。

  • Three first-author peer-reviewed papers and a doctoral dissertation形成 3 篇第一作者同行评议论文及博士学位论文
  • Continuous academic–industry collaboration with ANTA与 ANTA 持续开展产学合作
  • Biomechanics-informed lattice and footwear structure optimization面向生物力学目标的晶格与鞋具结构优化

Other Research & Knowledge Translation其他科研与知识转化

High-performance sport

Biomechanics Analysis for Elite Sport高水平竞技体育生物力学分析

Applied video-based human-movement analysis and computer vision to high-performance sport, including technical analysis for Zhejiang provincial swimming, the Chinese national ski-jumping team, and an Olympic weightlifting champion.

将视频人体运动分析和计算机视觉用于高水平竞技体育，曾为浙江省游泳队、中国国家跳台滑雪队及奥运会举重冠军提供技术分析。
